The problems

parser: dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first; all our tenants write the day first. Day and month swap: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.”

379 reports from 11 tenants: alder.physio (de-DE, booking-v3), copper.studio (es-ES, booking-v3), harbour.dental (en-GB, booking-v2), kettle.books (nl-NL, booking-v2), lantern.row (cs-CZ, booking-v2), long.table (it-IT, booking-v2), morrow.tax (fr-FR, booking-v2), nine.lanterns (pl-PL, booking-v2), pale.harbour (pt-PT, booking-v3), ridge.clinic (da-DK, booking-v2), stone.and.sons (en-IE, booking-v2).

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix will not touch it: the hint has to change.

33 reports from 3 tenants: alder.physio (de-DE, booking-v3), copper.studio (es-ES, booking-v3), pale.harbour (pt-PT, booking-v3).

one-day: bookings move by one day

These are not swaps. The booking lands one day early or late, or differently for attendees in different places (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). That points at time zones when the invite is built. The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json) and files them under the parser by tenant; the parser fix will not change them.

5 reports from 5 tenants: alder.physio (de-DE, booking-v3), kettle.books (nl-NL, booking-v2), lantern.row (cs-CZ, booking-v2), nine.lanterns (pl-PL, booking-v2), pale.harbour (pt-PT, booking-v3). Reports: R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620.
